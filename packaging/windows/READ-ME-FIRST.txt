LOOPWRIGHT FOR WINDOWS - HOW TO INSTALL
=======================================

1. Double-click Loopwright-Setup.exe

2. Windows may show a blue box: "Windows protected your PC".
   This is normal for new software from a small developer.
   Click  "More info"  and then  "Run anyway".

3. Click Yes when Windows asks for permission, then Next / Install.

4. Open your DAW, rescan plugins, and load Loopwright as an instrument
   (VST3 or CLAP). Or open the Loopwright app from the Start menu.

Your 7-day free trial starts the first time you open it.
Help: https://loop-wright.com/support  or  support@loop-wright.com
